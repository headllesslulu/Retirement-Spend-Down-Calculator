Retirement Spend-Down & Medicaid Transition

Retirement Spend-Down Calculator is a free, installable Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help individuals model their financial path from retirement through late life.

It includes projections for Social Security income, investment returns, inflation-adjusted spending, and Medicaid eligibility based on liquidity exhaustion.
